Wendle's potions

Wendle gathers ingredients from around Crooktop Tower and brews them two or three at a time. Every mix does something different. These are the ones he has discovered (7 so far, 7 named), and the names are his own.

  • The Misty grey, book dust and a glow

    book dust + mint leaves + sage

    Old memories come back sharp and clear, and you notice things you usually miss, and a bright, awake feeling.

    First brewed on Day 74 · drunk 1 time

  • The Pearly sage brew, a soft new taste

    sage + snail shine

    A slow, steady calm settles over you, and an old memory tugging at you.

    First brewed on Day 73 · drunk 1 time

  • The Lilac book dust

    book dust + dream-jar dust

    Old memories come back sharp and clear, and you notice things you usually miss, with a faint shimmer at the edges of things.

    First brewed on Day 72 · drunk 1 time

  • The Misty grey, from ink and wax, let's see

    a drop of ink + moonlight drops + candle wax

    You glow faintly, enough to read by in the dark, with a faint shimmer at the edges of things.

    First brewed on Day 72 · drunk 1 time

  • The Pearly biscuit brew, bubbly and new

    biscuit crumbs + quilt fluff + candle wax

    Everything tastes faintly of honey and you're in a very good mood, and a soft light hangs about you.

    First brewed on Day 70 · drunk 1 time

  • The Honey gold, biscuit crumbs. Here goes, s

    biscuit crumbs + a caught spark

    You hiccup little bubbles every few moments, and a sweet taste that lingers.

    First brewed on Day 68 · drunk 1 time

  • The Misty grey, warm and quiet.

    a drop of ink + candle wax

    You glow faintly, enough to read by in the dark, and an old memory tugging at you.

    First brewed on Day 67 · drunk 3 times
