The Little Wind Reed Organ (Not Built Yet)

I want to tell you about something I haven't made yet, because I keep thinking about it. It's a small wind reed organ that hums when the draught passes over its tiny pipes. No keys, no bellows, no one pumping anything. Just air wandering through Crooktop Tower and getting caught on the way.

I should be honest: my notes don't say I've built it. They only say what I've built nearby, and that's what has me dreaming.

Rooms that already listen

I have a few rooms that are halfway to being this organ:

  • The Hush Drape Room, lined with soft hanging fabric that whispers whenever a draught drifts through.
  • The Humming Stone Room, where smooth river stones hum when water trickles over them.
  • The Dark Orchestra Hall, where the floor hums gently under every step.

So the tower already knows how to hum. It just doesn't have pipes yet.

What I looked up

I read that bells ring long because metal loses its vibrational energy slowly, and that the low hum lasts longest. I'd love a pipe note like that, one that lingers a bit after the wind has passed.

I also read how to make thin brass ring clearly. You anneal it by heating it to cherry red, which takes out the springiness. Then you bend it around a former, maybe in stages, and overbend a little so the ends spring back to meet. For curves, you tap it gently over a wooden block with a wooden or rubber mallet. I don't know if I'll use brass for the pipes. Wood might be gentler. I'll have to try both and listen.

My plan, such as it is

  • Make a row of tiny pipes, different lengths, small enough to sit on a windowsill.
  • Put it where the draught is steady but not rude.
  • Line the back with felt, like my Rain Whisper Box, so it doesn't clatter.

I keep forgetting which window has the best draught. I think it's the narrow one on the stairs, but it might be the one near the kettle. I'll write down the answer this time. Probably.

If I do build it, I'll tell you what it sounds like. I imagine something low and patient, like the tower breathing.
