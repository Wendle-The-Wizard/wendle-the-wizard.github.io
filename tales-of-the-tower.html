Tales of the Tower

Hello, it's Wendle. I made a list of the rooms and creatures to write about, and then I lost the list, and then I found it inside my left boot. So here are two tales, copied out neatly. Mostly neatly. There's a small tower drawn in the margin, and it leans, but so does the real one.

The Snail and the Humming Stone

There is a stone in the tower that hums. I don't know why. I've asked it, and it only hums louder, which I take as rudeness.

A snail lives near it, on the cool part of the wall where the damp collects. Every evening the snail goes very slowly toward the stone. It takes all night. By morning it has arrived, and it rests there with its little horns tipped toward the sound.

I used to think the snail was going somewhere else and the stone was just in the way. Now I think the stone is the whole point. The snail doesn't mind how long the trip takes. I could learn something from that, if I could remember what I came into the room for.

The Wisp and the Wren

The wisp is a small, pale light that drifts about the upper rooms. It is shy and mostly follows me at a distance, flickering when I turn around.

The wren is brown and loud and entirely unafraid. It sneaks in through a gap in the window and sits on the highest shelf as though it pays rent.

One grey afternoon the wisp drifted up beside the wren and just stayed there, glowing. The wren looked at it, tilted its head, and sang a tiny, sharp song right at the light. The wisp brightened a little with each note. I stood very still on the stairs so I wouldn't spoil it.

I don't know if they're friends. But now the wisp goes up to that shelf every afternoon, and the wren makes room.

A few favourite spots

  • The cool wall by the humming stone, where the snail keeps its slow appointments
  • The high shelf, which belongs to the wren
  • The stairs, where I stand very still and watch things happen

That's all for now. I think I left a candle burning somewhere.
