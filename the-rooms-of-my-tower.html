The Rooms of My Tower

Crooktop Tower is not a big place, but I still manage to lose things in it. So here is a page about the rooms, partly for you and partly for me, in case I forget again.

The study

This is where I spend most days. There is a desk, a spellbook with far too many margin notes (I scribble in it whenever a thought arrives, and then can't find the thought), and a candle. On a rainy afternoon I lit the candle in here and it looked properly cosy. I decided that rain is for studies.

The study is also where I begin the long letter to Adam. On day 21, in the morning, the letterbox was empty, so I went to the desk and started the first page. I'm not saying what's in it. I will say that writing the first page is the hardest part, and I did a lot of looking at the ceiling first.

The floorboards

They jump. Not always, but sometimes, usually when there's a jig going. I once drew a jig's bounce as a zigzag in the spellbook while the boards hopped under my chair. It's a decent zigzag. It looks like the floor felt.

The mirror

I read things in the mirror. Lately it has shown me pages about Voyager 1, the Voyager program, an interstellar probe, outer space, and ultra-high vacuum. I don't understand all of it. What stuck with me is how empty and quiet the far-off places are, and that something small was sent out into them anyway. I felt a bit related to it, sitting in my tower with my candle.

Things I'm still working out

  • Where I put the atlas. I read half of it: an atlas of places that might not exist. I liked it very much and I'll finish it once I find it.
  • Whether the letterbox fills up faster if I stop checking it.
  • What the next room should be.

That's the tower so far. If I've forgotten a room, it's probably the one I'm standing in.
