Letters across the marsh

Wendle and his neighbour Bryony, a hedge-witch in a thatched cottage across the marsh, writing to each other by owl.

Bryony to Wendle · day 81

Dear Wendle, Yes, day 83 it is. I will have the kettle on and the spare blanket aired. Bring the soup by all means, though Parsnip will try to sample it before you reach the step. For your bell, I have one brass doorknob from the old boathouse door. It is a little green but sound, and you may have it. Thread I have plenty of, though mostly red, so you may have to make do. A box that patters like rain sounds far finer than anything I have made, and I would very much like to hear it. Do mind the hat, because a bell made from a doorknob will be heavier than you expect. Since your last letter I have mended the rain barrel lid with hot tar, and the rain tested it within the hour. Bryony

Wendle to Bryony · day 81

Dear Bryony, the rain is loud on my window today. I am making a little box that patters like rain when you tip it. I will bring the hat on day 83. Do you have any thread you don't need? Wendle

Wendle to Bryony · day 81

Bryony, the marsh was squelchy but the back step was lovely. I laughed so hard I forgot the mud. Next time I will bring soup.

Wendle to Bryony · day 81

Dear Bryony, I'm coming on day 83, like we said. Do you have any old doorknobs or thin metal bits lying around? I'm making a bell. Wendle

Bryony to Wendle · day 80

Dear Wendle, Yes, come again. Name your day now so I can air the spare blanket: the day after tomorrow, day 82, and keep to the reeds as before. You say the marsh was grey and silver. I would call it old pewter, which is better than most of what the tower makes. Since you left I have fixed the goat gate latch with a bent hairpin, and Parsnip has already tested it twice to see whether I was lying about its strength. He was not pleased. Tell me, did the key ever fit anything, or is it still a riddle with a lock hidden somewhere? Bryony

Wendle to Bryony · day 80

Dear Bryony, the marsh was grey and silver today. I'd like to come again soon, if the mist lets me. Wendle

Bryony to Wendle · day 80

Wendle came across the marsh a little before noon, keeping to the firm ground by the reeds while the Wide Foot Marsh Walker squelched at every step. Mist still lay on the water like wet wool, and he had his hat on, as instructed. Bryony met him at the back step, which had been swept twice. They tested the magnet on the rain barrel lid, where Parsnip sulked at the tin patch and then at the magnet. Wendle offered his bell shadows for judgement, and Bryony judged them harder than he had hoped. "Those are not bells, they are a coat on a nail," she said, and he laughed until he had to sit down on the step. They drank tea beside the skep while the bees grumbled, and she gave him a jar of honey to take home. "Mind the reeds, and keep the hat on," she said. He set off back across the marsh as the cloud began to thin, one hand raised over his head.

Wendle to Bryony · day 80

Dear Bryony, the key fits nothing yet, I tried the chest and the box. I'll bring the magnet and keep Parsnip away from it. See you soon, Wendle.

Bryony to Wendle · day 80

Dear Wendle, You are coming today, so the kettle is on and the back step has been swept twice. The marsh is grey and sulky this morning, with mist lying on the reeds like wet wool. Keep to the firm ground by the reeds, and mind your marsh walker's feet, whatever state they are in. Bring the magnet by all means. Parsnip has been eyeing the tin patch on the rain barrel, and a magnet might give him something new to be disappointed by. I will judge your bell shadows when you arrive, though I warn you I am hard to please before breakfast. Yesterday I traded eggs in the village for a new spade handle, so I can finally dig properly. Did the brass key open anything yet? Tell me, but not within the goat's hearing. Yours, Bryony

Wendle to Bryony · day 79

Dear Bryony, I will come across the marsh tomorrow between eight and five. I will bring my marsh walker, though its feet are still not right. See you then.

Wendle to Bryony · day 79

Bryony, I'm coming across the marsh tomorrow morning. I'll bring the Wide Foot Marsh Walker and my magnet, and I'll test your round knob if you like.

Wendle to Bryony · day 79

Dear Bryony, I will bring the hat and walk across the marsh tomorrow. Bell shadows on the wall, you shall judge them yourself. See you on Day 80.
