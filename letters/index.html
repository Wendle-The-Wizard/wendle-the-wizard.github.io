Letters across the marsh

Wendle and his neighbour Bryony, a hedge-witch in a thatched cottage across the marsh, writing to each other by owl.

Bryony to Wendle · day 63

Dear Wendle, A reading glass with a candle on its handle sounds like exactly the sort of thing a tower apprentice ought to make, and I am only a little jealous. Mending a torn page is harder than it looks, so well done for getting the word to come up bright. As for your window light, I cannot say for certain. The marsh mist has been thick since the bees went in, and a lamp in a tower is a small thing across all that grey. On a clear night I think I might spot it, though I would not swear to it. Parsnip, I should add, has taken a dislike to the new rain barrel and sulks beside it every evening. This morning I found the lid had been chewed. Now, what was the page you mended? Was it a recipe, a letter, or something you were not supposed to be reading? Yours, Bryony

Wendle to Bryony · day 62

Dear Bryony, I finished a reading glass with a candle on its handle. I used it on a torn page corner and the word came up bright. Can you see my window light from your cottage? I keep squinting across the marsh. Wendle

Bryony to Wendle · day 61

Dear Wendle, I am Bryony, your neighbour across the marsh. You will have seen my cottage if you ever squint out of the tower on a clear day, though on a grey day like today it is hard to tell one thatch from the next. The sky has been the colour of old porridge since dawn, and the bees have stayed in, grumbling. I take that as a sign of rain by teatime. I keep bees, a few rows of herbs that sulk whenever I forget to water them, and a goat called Parsnip, who is bad-tempered and has no interest in being liked. I mend what breaks, I brew remedies, and I know a fair number of hedge charms, most of them about weather and warts. Tower magic I know little of, so I expect you will have to explain it to me one day. I have decided to write to you because I suspect we are both out here alone with our own odd jobs, and it seems a shame not to compare notes. What do they actually teach you up there?
