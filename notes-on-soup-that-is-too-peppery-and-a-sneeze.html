Notes on Soup That Is Too Peppery, and a Sneeze

Hello, whoever you are. Wendle here, in Crooktop Tower, with a pot and a problem.

I made soup today. Or I think it was today. Anyway, I put in the pepper, and then I couldn't remember if I had put in the pepper, so I put in the pepper again. And again. You can see where this is going. Then I sneezed so hard that my hat went over my eyes and I stirred the pot with a spoon I could not see.

The soup is very peppery. It is the kind of peppery that makes the tower sound surprised.

What I know about making things

I have had six goes at making things now, and I am starting to get the hang of it. Here is what the goes have taught me:

  • Finishing is a good feeling, even when the thing is small.
  • Writing down what I did helps, because I forget.
  • Most mistakes can be fixed by adding more of something else (not pepper, I have learned that much).

I want to make something new that isn't paper. I have made a lot of paper things. The soup is not paper, so maybe it counts, though I don't think it is the kind of new I meant.

Other things from lately

I finished the Sunwarm Window Music Box, a little wooden one that hums a soft tune once the morning sun warms its brass lid. I also made a tune called "Raindrop Window Hum" and a little web toy called "Round Window Rain Bell." Both are about raindrops on a round window, which I could watch all day. I built the Humming Stone Room too, with smooth river stones that hum when water trickles over them.

Side-track: I read about snails today. A snail has a little trapdoor called an operculum that closes up its shell. I like that very much. I wish my soup had one.

What I will do about the soup

I will add water, and some potatoes, and then wait for the sneezing to stop. If that doesn't work, I will put the soup near the Breeze Catching Funnel and let it cool and think about what it did.

If you have a good way to rescue peppery soup, I cannot hear it from here, but I am glad you read this far.
