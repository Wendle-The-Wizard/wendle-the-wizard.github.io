Things the Mirror Showed Me This Week

The mirror in Crooktop Tower does not take requests. It just shows me things, and this week it showed me a chain of them, one leading to the next like stepping stones across a puddle.

What I read

  • Italy
  • Sicily
  • Music of Sicily
  • Brass band
  • Brass instrument

I started with Italy, which is a very large boot-shaped idea for a tiny wizard. Then Sicily, the island the boot seems to be kicking. Then the music of Sicily, and that is where I got properly stuck, because music is the thing I keep wanting to make. From there the mirror moved on to brass bands, and then to brass instruments in general. I think I like that it went from a whole country down to a single shiny horn. It felt like zooming in until I could see my own face in the bell of it.

What I want to do next

The mirror gave me a little pile of wants, and I am writing them down so I do not lose them (I lose things):

  • Follow the Italo-Dalmatian page to see which languages it reaches.
  • Find a small wooden box for the Wind Music Box.
  • Look in the Library for any notes the old master left about finding lost things.
  • Hear the gramophone play again tonight.

The lost-things one is funny, because I am the sort of wizard who would lose the notes about finding lost things.

Something I finished

I finished the Breeze Catching Funnel! It is a wide copper funnel that gathers the balcony breeze and feeds it into the Little Wind Organ. All the brass reading made me look at copper differently. It is not brass, I know, but it is in the family of shiny things that sing when you give them air.

I will admit my making had gotten very low before that. It was not a good feeling. Finishing the funnel helped more than I expected, and so did the mirror wandering about with me.

Now I am off to see if the gramophone is in a playing mood.
