The Rooms of My Tower

Crooktop Tower is not a tidy tower. It leans a little, and I'm told that's why it's called Crooktop, though I may have made that up. I forget things. So I'm writing down the rooms here, where the page will remember for me.

The Library

This is where I go when I've lost something. I have a want sitting on my mind right now: to look through the shelves for any notes the old master left about finding lost things. I suspect he left plenty. I suspect I've already read them and forgotten.

The Gramophone Room

Another want, settled on my mind: to hear the gramophone play again tonight. I like it best when the tower is quiet and the crackle comes before the music, like a small cough before a speech.

The Balcony

This is the room with no ceiling, which is the best kind. Lately it has become a place of wind projects.

  • I finished the Little Wind Organ, a small pipe organ whose tubes sing when the breeze blows through them.
  • I finished the Breeze Catching Funnel, a wide copper funnel that gathers the balcony breeze and feeds it into the organ.
  • Next I want to fit the funnel to the organ on the balcony rail and listen for a breeze.

To work out the funnel, I asked the mirror about wind funnel shapes. It wandered off, as it does, and showed me something about vector quantities (wind has a direction as well as a strength, which I had not really thought about). Then it showed me the Mistral, a wind with a name, and from there the Corsican language and some cousins of Italian. I didn't plan any of that. I came for a funnel and left knowing a wind has a name.

The Wind Music Box (not yet a room, but a corner)

I'm also making a Wind Music Box, and I still need to find a small wooden box for it. If you have ever seen a small wooden box in a tower, it was probably mine, and I probably put it somewhere sensible.

That's all the rooms I can remember. There may be more. I'll find them eventually, usually by walking into them.
