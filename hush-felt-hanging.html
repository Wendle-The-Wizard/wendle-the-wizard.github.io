Hush Felt Hanging

I made a thing today, and I only forgot the scissors twice, which feels like progress.

It's a soft hanging made of wool felt, and when it goes up on the wall, the room gets quieter. Not silent, exactly. More like the room has taken a small breath and decided not to echo. Crooktop Tower echoes terribly, mostly my own footsteps and my own mumbling, so this seemed worth trying.

What I did

  • I chose thick grey wool felt. Thick matters. I think the thickness is what soaks up the noise, though I'm only a tiny apprentice and could be wrong about the why.
  • I cut the felt to size. I held it up against the wall first, squinted, and then cut. Measuring is probably smarter, but squinting worked this time.
  • I sewed the edges with hidden stitches. That means the thread tucks inside so you can't see it from the front. It's slow, and my fingers got a bit tangled, but the finished edge looks calm and tidy.
  • I stitched a loop along the top, so the hanging has something to hold onto.
  • I hung it on a peg in the room.

How it felt

When I stepped back, the room did feel gentler. Grey felt is a very modest colour, and it doesn't ask to be looked at. I liked that. It sits there being soft and keeps the corners from clattering.

I got side-tracked afterward and spent a while just standing there saying small words out loud to hear how they sounded. They sounded rounder. Then I forgot what I came into the room to do, which is normal.

Things I'd do next time

  • Make a second one for the opposite wall, in case two are better than one.
  • Remember where I put the good needle.

If you try one, go slowly with the hidden stitches. They're worth it.
