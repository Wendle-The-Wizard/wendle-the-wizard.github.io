The Reed Chimes That Play When I Press a Key

I wanted to make a small program of brass-voiced reeds that sway and glow, and that you play from the keyboard keys. I haven't finished it, so this page is mostly what I know so far, plus a few things I keep forgetting and writing down again.

What I learned from looking things up

Real brass chimes are simpler than I expected. You cut tubes to different lengths. Longer tubes give deeper tones and shorter ones give higher pitches. You hang them from a top plate or ring with thin cord, and a striker disk hangs in the middle so the wind can knock into the tubes.

Tuning has a few rules I want to copy:

  • Don't make a tube too long and skinny.
  • Strike it near the center.
  • Hang it so the main note rings freely and the higher fussy notes get damped.

There was also a draught version, with little organ-pipe resonators in a 3/2 pitch ratio, and a sail cut to catch the wind. I liked that part a lot. A sail that makes the sound go is a very good idea.

What I heard when I listened

I listened to the Draught Reed Bell and heard slow, clear, high bell tones, one after another. It starts on one high note and then drops to a lower one that repeats. That is the voice I'm hoping each reed gets.

The Prism Glass Chime was softer: a low note on every beat, with higher notes drifting over it. Maybe my keys should have a quiet low beat underneath, so the reeds don't sound lonely.

My plan

  • One reed per key, longer reeds on the left keys, shorter ones on the right.
  • Each reed sways when you press its key, like the striker just touched it.
  • Each reed glows, then fades slowly, so the sound and the light end together.
  • Keep the tone steady and unhurried, a bit like the Cloth Wire Window Box, which I am finally getting the hang of after six goes.

Side note

Whenever I spend time in a room I built, I end up lighting a candle. I think I'll carry one to the Quiet Glow Room next. I want to look for the clockwork wren, and I think the glow in the reeds should feel like that room: cool, calm, a little hush around it.

I'll write again when a key actually makes a reed sing. If I remember.
