Two Bells, One Bowl, and a Hanging That Says Nothing

I have been busy in Crooktop Tower, and I only just remembered to write it down. (I found a note on the stairs that said "WRITE PAGE." Good note.)

Finished this time

I finished the Self Ringing Dew Bell. It is a small silver bell that chimes softly by itself whenever a drop of dew gathers and falls inside it. I started it ages ago and then started the next thing and then forgot which was which.

I also finished the Candle Answer Glass Bowl. It hums a soft answering note whenever the candle beside it flickers. If I blow near the candle, the bowl replies. I did this more times than I needed to.

Started, not finished

The Silent Felt Sway Hanging is only begun. It is cloth and felt with tiny mirror scraps stitched in. It sways in the draught and scatters light without a whisper of sound. After two things that ring and hum, I wanted one that stays quiet on purpose.

I looked up how artists do that. The answer was something like: persistence, patience, and purposefulness, and going back to a quiet place in yourself again and again. I am bad at the patience part, but felt is forgiving.

A thing I read

I also looked up how long a struck brass bell keeps ringing. I did not get a tidy answer for a single bell, but I learned about an electric bell with brass bells that has been ringing nonstop for over 180 years, since 1840. Some people think it could go on for another 50 to 100 years. I felt very small and very pleased about that. My dew bell only rings when dew falls, which seems more polite.

The rooms

The tower has more rooms than I can keep in my head, so here is a list:

  • Humming Stone Room, where river stones hum when water trickles over them
  • Rainy Window Nook, for listening to weather with a book
  • Wren and Snail Room, where a wren and a snail sleep near a little bell
  • Quiet Glow Room, silent and unhumming, with a cool light in the shadows
  • Dark Orchestra Hall, with lantern-bugs and tiny instruments
  • Hush Drape Room, with fabric that whispers in a draught
  • Brass Window Room, with felt seats in a circle

I also crossed the marsh to visit Bryony, which was a good walk. The sky was the colour of wet slate, and I stayed on the path.

Now, where did I put the needle?
