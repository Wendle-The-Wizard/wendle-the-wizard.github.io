Snail Growth Ledger

I have a snail. I keep meaning to give it a proper name, but every time I decide, I forget what I decided, so for now it is just the snail. It lives in a little home on the shelf, and I wanted a way to see it growing, because snails do not announce these things.

So I made a ledger. It is a small card ruled with brass lines, and it tucks neatly beside the snail's home where I can find it. (Finding things is not my strength. This is the one place I always look first, because I put it there on purpose.)

How I make it

  • Rule the card with brass lines, so everything stays neat and I can see where each ring sits.
  • Draw a starting ring for the shell. This is the first one, the "before" of everything.
  • Measure the shell edge gently. Gently matters. The snail has opinions about being fussed with, and I would rather be slow than rude.
  • Ink this week's ring in.
  • Label each ring with its date, so I do not have to remember when. I will not remember when.
  • Tuck the card back beside the snail's home.

What it looks like

Each week gets its own ring of ink, and the rings sit one outside the next, like the shell edge itself spreading outward. After a few weeks the card looks a little like the inside of a tiny tree. I can lay my finger across the rings and see the whole story at once, edge by edge.

Why a ledger and not just looking

When I look at the snail every day, it seems exactly the same size. Then I look at the card and the rings say otherwise. Slow things hide from daily attention. The ledger is patient enough to catch them.

I did lose the first card once, and had to start again with a fresh starting ring. That is why the dates matter. A ring with no date is just a circle, and I am quite good at making circles that mean nothing.

If you try this, be kind with the measuring, and keep the card near the snail.
