Notes on a Cool Glow, So Far

I wanted to make something new that isn't another hum or chime. I've made a lot of those. The Paper Draught Chime hums when the stair draught drifts by, and the Humming Stone Room hums when water trickles over the stones. Lovely, but I wanted a change. So I went looking for glow, and these are my notes so far. I haven't made anything with it yet, and I might forget half of this by Tuesday.

What I found out

Fireflies make light without making much heat. I looked it up twice, because the first answer left me wondering why they don't burn.

Here is the recipe, as best I understand it:

  • Inside special organs in the firefly's abdomen, a substance called luciferin meets oxygen.
  • An enzyme called luciferase speeds the meeting up.
  • ATP, which is a kind of energy the body carries around, powers the whole thing.

This is called bioluminescence. The surprising part is that over 90% of the energy becomes visible light, and less than 10% turns into heat. My hearth is the opposite. Most of its energy becomes heat, and the light is a bonus. I watched the clay dish change colour in that fire, and it was bright but very much not cool.

Where I got side-tracked

I also have a question I haven't answered: why do beetle shells shine? I suspect it's a different sort of shine, because a shell doesn't seem to be mixing chemicals. Maybe it's about how light bounces off it. I don't know yet, so I won't pretend to. A firefly is a beetle, though, which tangles my thoughts nicely. One kind of beetle makes its own glow, and others only shine when light hits them.

What I might do next

I don't know how to make luciferin, and I'm fairly sure I shouldn't try in a tower with a draughty stair. But I could build a room around the idea of a cool glow. Maybe a dark little nook, like the Rainy Window Nook but for evenings, where something soft glows and nothing gets warm.

For now I'm keeping a list of questions:

  • Why do beetle shells shine?
  • Can any glow be as cool as a firefly's?
  • Where did I put my notes about the music box orchestra?

I'll write again when I know more.
