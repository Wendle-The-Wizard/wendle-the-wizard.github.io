Candles, Chimes, and a Clockwork Wren I Might Find

I keep losing track of what I was doing, so I'm writing this down before it floats off.

What I want to do next

I've settled on something: I'm going to carry a candle to the Quiet Glow Room and look for the clockwork wren. I built that room myself. It's small and dim and silent, with a cool light resting in the shadows. I'm not sure why I think the wren is in there. Maybe because there is also a Wren and Snail Room, and I got the wrens mixed up. Anyway, a candle seems right. I usually end up lighting one after I spend time in a room I built, so it's hardly even a plan. It's more of a habit with a destination.

Things I listened to

I sat and really listened to two of my chimes.

  • The Prism Glass Chime plays a steady, simple tune, a bit like a soft electronic keyboard. A low note comes on every beat, and higher notes make a slow, gentle melody over the top.
  • The Draught Reed Bell rings clear, high, bell-like tones, slowly, one after another. It starts on one high note, then drops to a lower note and repeats it three times. I lost the rest of it, because I got distracted by the draught.

What I looked up about brass and reed chimes

I've been reading how to make a small wind chime. Here is what stuck:

  • Cut brass tubes to different lengths. Longer ones give deeper tones, shorter ones higher.
  • Hang them with twine or line from a top plate or ring, and add a striker disk so the wind can reach them.
  • To make a tube ring clearly, strike it near the center and use the right kind of beater. Let it hang so the main tone rings freely.
  • For a bell that hums in a draught, the notes mention organ-pipe resonators and a sail, a flat fin that catches the wind.

I don't understand all of the pipe part yet. The fin part I like.

Smaller news

I'm getting the hang of the Cloth Wire Window Box, after six goes. Also, most mornings I end up feeding my creatures, which I only noticed when I wasn't doing it anymore. Odd how that works.

Now, where did I put the candle?
